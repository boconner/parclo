What is Parclo?

Parclo is the field operations platform built for Contento. It's where you track the bottles on shelf at every store in your territory, log every visit you make, and coordinate tastings and events — all in one place.

Management uses Parclo to see territory health in real time. The data you enter is the data they see. Accurate, timely logging makes your work visible and helps the whole team move faster.

What you'll use it for

  • Checking inventory levels before a store visit
  • Logging visits with on-shelf counts
  • Managing tasting and event schedules
  • Viewing store contacts

How it helps you

  • See which stores need attention today
  • Know your delivery status at a glance
  • Never miss an upcoming event
  • Build a complete record of every store interaction

Getting Set Up & Logging In

Step 1 — Accept Your Invite

Before you can log in, your manager will send you an invitation email. Look for an email with Contento in the subject line — this is your account invite with a link to set your password.

Check Your Spam

The invite email sometimes lands in your spam or junk folder. If you don't see a Contento email in your inbox within a few minutes of your manager sending it, check spam before asking for a resend.

  1. Find the Contento invite email in your inbox (or spam folder).
  2. Click the link in the email to open the account setup page.
  3. Set a secure password and complete your profile.
  4. You'll be redirected to the Contento app automatically.

Step 2 — Log In

Once your account is set up, you can log in any time at:

app.parclo.io
  1. Go to app.parclo.io in any browser — Chrome, Safari, and Edge all work.
  2. Enter your work email address and password.
  3. You'll land on the Dashboard — your home base.

Tip

Bookmark app.parclo.io and add it to your phone's home screen for one-tap access. Contento is fully mobile-friendly.

Password Reset

Tap your profile avatar in the top-right corner and select Change Password at any time.

The Dashboard

The Dashboard is your daily starting point. It shows a live snapshot of every store in your assigned territory.

What You See

Your dashboard shows two panels side by side:

  • Region Coverage — a map of your territory with color-coded pins for every store. Red pins are critical (≤ 3 bottles), amber are needs attention (4 bottles), green are good (5+).
  • Store Inventory — a table listing all your stores with their current on-shelf count, status, and last visit date. Click any row to open that store's full detail page.

Below the map and table you'll see Active Alerts — flagged stores that need rep action — and the Activity Feed showing recent visits and events across your territory.

Notification Bell

The bell icon in the top navigation bar shows a red badge when stores need urgent attention. Click it to see:

  • Critical Stores — stores with 3 or fewer bottles on shelf
  • Needs Attention — stores with exactly 4 bottles on shelf

Click either row to open a full list of those stores with one-tap navigation to each store's detail page.

Daily Habit

Check the notification bell every morning. Critical stores should be visited before end of day. Alerts clear automatically when you log a visit with an updated bottle count.

Understanding Inventory Status

Every store in Parclo has a color-coded status based on bottles currently on shelf. You'll see these throughout the app — on the dashboard, store list, and map pins.

Critical 3 or fewer bottles on shelf. Needs a visit immediately. ≤ 3 bottles
Needs Attention Exactly 4 bottles on shelf. Plan a visit soon. 4 bottles
Good 5 or more bottles on shelf. Inventory is healthy. 5+ bottles

The Days of Supply column shows how many days of stock remain at the store's current depletion rate. This number counts down daily so it's always up to date — even without a new visit.

Note

Status is based on bottles physically on shelf. Only a logged visit with an updated count will move a store out of Critical.

Logging a Store Visit

Logging a visit is the most important thing you do in Contento. It updates the store's on-shelf count, timestamps your activity, and gives management a real-time picture of territory health.

How to Log a Visit

  1. Tap the + Log button in the top-right of the Dashboard — or open the store's detail page and tap Log Visit there.
  2. Select the Store you visited from the dropdown.
  3. Enter the Bottles On Shelf count — the physical count you see right now.
  4. Set the Visit Type to match what you did (see table below).
  5. Optionally link a Calendar Event if this visit was tied to a scheduled tasting or store event. Linking it closes out the event automatically.
  6. Add Notes, Takeaways, and Accomplishments — notes are required; takeaways and accomplishments appear in reports.
  7. Enter Hours Worked — the time spent on this visit. This field is required on every log.
  8. Tap Save Visit. The store's inventory is updated instantly.

Visit Types

Type When to use it
Stocked You physically restocked shelves — bottles moved from back stock to shelf.
Checked You visited and counted inventory but didn't need to restock.
Issue Reported You found a problem — product damage, display issue, pricing error, etc.

Best Practice

Always count the bottles physically on the shelf at the time of your visit — not what's in the back room. The on-shelf count is what customers see and what drives the status color.

Linking a Calendar Event

If you're logging a visit that was tied to a scheduled tasting or store event, use the Link Calendar Event field in the visit form. This:

  • Automatically marks the event as Completed
  • Captures bottles sold and hours worked from the event
  • Prevents duplicate entries in your activity history

Hours Worked

Hours Worked is required on every visit log — not just tastings. Enter the total time spent at the store, including setup and travel time at the location. This feeds directly into the rep performance report that management reviews monthly.

Required Field

You won't be able to save a visit without entering Hours Worked. If you're unsure of the exact time, enter your best estimate — an accurate record matters more than a perfect number.

Store Detail Page

Click any store name — from the Dashboard, the Stores page, or a notification — to open its detail page. This is the full history and current status of a single store.

What you'll find here

  • Current inventory — on shelf, days of supply
  • Visit history — every logged visit with notes
  • Active alerts — any flagged issues
  • Contacts — buyer and manager contacts for this store
  • Linked events — past tastings at this location

Actions you can take

  • Log a new visit directly from this page
  • View or call a store contact
  • See the store's location on the map
  • Review your notes from previous visits

Calendar & Events

The Calendar shows all scheduled and past events across your territory. There are three event types:

Event Type Description Examples
Tasting An in-store consumer tasting event. Tied to a specific store. Tracks bottles sold. Saturday afternoon tasting at a wine shop
Store Visit (Event) A scheduled merchandising or support visit to a store. Tied to a specific store. Tracks hours worked. End-cap setup, staff training, display build
Private Event An off-site or venue event not tied to a specific store. Has its own address. Tracks bottles sold and hours. Restaurant launch, corporate tasting, festival, private party

Closing Out an Event

  1. After completing an event, open the Calendar page and find your event.
  2. Click the event to open its detail panel.
  3. Tap Close Out and fill in bottles sold, hours worked, notes, and takeaways.
  4. Alternatively, close it out by logging a store visit and linking this event — both the visit and event will be marked complete in one step.

Tip

Always close out events promptly. Bottles sold at tastings feed into the rep performance report — unclosed events mean your numbers are understated.

Contacts

The Contacts section stores buyers, managers, and decision-makers for your stores and chains.

  • Access contacts from the Contacts link in the sidebar, or from within a Store's detail page.
  • Each contact shows their role, phone number, email, and which stores or chains they're linked to.
  • Tap a phone number to call directly from your mobile device.
  • Contacts linked to a chain appear across all stores in that chain.

Adding Contacts

If a contact is missing, you can add them from the Contacts page. Fill in their name, role, and link them to the appropriate store or chain. Your manager can also assign contacts in bulk.

Best Practices & Quick Reference

Daily Checklist

  • Check the notification bell for critical stores
  • Review your calendar for today's events
  • Log every store visit the same day you make it

Weekly Habits

  • Visit any store that hasn't been seen in 7 days
  • Close out all completed events
  • Scan your Store Inventory table for stores last visited 10+ days ago

Quick Reference: Where Is Everything?

I want to… Go to…
Log a store visit Dashboard → + Log button, or any Store Detail page
See which stores are critical Bell icon in the header → Critical Stores
View a store's full history Stores → select a chain → click any store row
Check my upcoming events Calendar page in the sidebar
Call a store buyer Contacts page, or Store Detail → Contacts tab
Close out a tasting or event Calendar → click the event → Close Out
See all my past visits Visit Log in the sidebar

Golden Rule

If it happened in the field, it should be in Parclo. Every visit, every count — logged same day. Your territory data is only as good as what you put in.
